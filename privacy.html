Privacy Policy

Simple Screen Snap Tool is designed to work entirely on your Mac. It does not collect, store or share any of your personal data.

Effective date: October 18, 2026

In short

  • No personal data is collected.
  • The app makes no network connections.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or third-party SDKs.
  • No account or sign-in.
  • Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

Information we collect

None. Simple Screen Snap Tool does not collect, transmit or sell any information about you or how you use the app. The developer has no servers that receive data from the app and cannot see your screenshots or annotations.

Screenshots and annotations

Screenshots you take and the annotations you add are processed only in your Mac's memory. They are written to disk only when you choose to save them (by default to ~/Pictures/Simple Screen Snap Tool, or a location you pick with Save As…), or placed on the clipboard when you copy them. What happens to an image after that — for example, pasting it into another app — is up to you.

Settings

Your preferences, such as the save resolution, are stored locally on your Mac using the standard macOS preferences system. They are never sent anywhere.

Permissions

The app asks for macOS Screen Recording permission because macOS requires it to capture the contents of your screen. It is used only when you start a capture. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording.

Network, analytics and third parties

The app contains no networking code that sends data, and it includes no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking libraries. It works fully offline. Because no data is collected, nothing is shared with or sold to third parties.

Crash reports

If the app crashes and you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in macOS (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the developer with anonymized crash reports. This is controlled entirely by you and by Apple; the app itself does not send them. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
