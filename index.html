Support

Simple Screen Snap Tool lives in your menu bar. Capture any part of your screen, mark it up with arrows, shapes, text and pixelation, then copy it or save it — all on your Mac, with no internet connection required.

Works fully offline No account Data Not Collected

Getting started

  1. Open Simple Screen Snap Tool. Its icon appears in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
  2. Press ⇧ ⌘ 9 or use the menu bar icon to start a capture.
  3. The first time, macOS asks for Screen Recording permission. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording, turn on Simple Screen Snap Tool, then quit and reopen the app if macOS asks you to.
  4. Drag to select an area, annotate it with the toolbar, then press Return to copy it to the clipboard and close, or ⌘ S to save it.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my screenshots saved?

By default, ⌘ S saves to ~/Pictures/Simple Screen Snap Tool. Use ⇧ ⌘ S (Save As…) to pick a different name or location for a single image.

Why does the app need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires this permission for any app that captures what is on your screen. The app uses it only to take the screenshot you ask for. Nothing is recorded in the background and nothing leaves your Mac. You can turn the permission off at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording.

I granted permission but capturing still doesn't work.

Quit Simple Screen Snap Tool from its menu bar icon and open it again. macOS applies a newly granted Screen Recording permission only after the app restarts. If it still fails, remove the app from the Screen Recording list, add it again, and restart the app.

My screenshots are very large. Can I save them at normal size?

Yes. On Retina displays, screenshots are captured at full pixel resolution (2x) by default. In the app's Settings you can choose to save images at standard (1x) resolution instead, which makes files smaller and matches the size you see on screen.

Does the app upload my screenshots anywhere?

No. There is no cloud service, no account and no sync. The app makes no network connections; your screenshots stay on your Mac unless you share them yourself. See the Privacy Policy.
